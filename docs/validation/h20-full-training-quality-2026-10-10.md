## Training and version evidence

- DINOv2-with-registers base encoder and ViT-XL decoder, 256×256 images, ImageNet-1k training set, 16 epochs and 40,032 generator steps.
- Eight H20 GPUs, global batch 512, per-GPU batch 64, no gradient accumulation; `--precision fp32` with the recorded optimization preset, including local BF16 operations and TF32.
- Upstream schedule: reconstruction/LPIPS from epoch 0, discriminator updates from epoch 6, adversarial generator loss from epoch 8; EMA decay 0.9978.
- The training completion log records the final checkpoint at epoch 16. The checkpoint inspection records epoch 16 / step 40,032 and a finite, compatible EMA decoder with 456 tensors and approximately 415.4M parameters.
- All **73 files** in [the original source manifest](../source-snapshot.json) match the H20 `harness_final` tree byte-for-byte, including the optimization preset. All **50 Python files** in the saved training-run source copy also match that tree.

## Evaluation protocol

Reconstruction uses upstream commit `a4d18c4db766419cbe7cb8c02cd9f7ceb0ec9041`, with all optimization switches unset, `stage1_sample_ddp.py`, eight GPUs, per-GPU batch 4, `--precision fp32`, and its default TF32 matmul setting. It loads the final EMA decoder and the standard DINOv2-B pretrained configuration. Images use ADM center cropping at 256×256. Both packed references and reconstructions contain 50,000 uint8 RGB images in the same sorted order.

PSNR, SSIM and LPIPS use the upstream `src/eval` implementation. rFID is calculated against the packed validation images with both the ADM evaluator and torch-fidelity; the README reports ADM. Evaluation completed successfully on October 10. Data preparation scripts identify the retained ImageNet parquet files as downloads from the public ModelScope `ILSVRC/imagenet-1k` dataset.

Only aggregate results and provenance are published in [the JSON record](h20-full-training-quality-2026-10-10.json). Checkpoints, datasets and reconstructions remain on the training machine. The separate `goal_final` experiment is not the snapshot released in this repository, so its metrics are not used for Faster-RAE.
